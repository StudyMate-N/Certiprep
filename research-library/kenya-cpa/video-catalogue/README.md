## Study sequence and gaps

Foundation: use OpenTuition FA for bookkeeping, NYU accounting for statement interpretation, MIT microeconomics for economic concepts, MIT probability for probability, and CS50 SQL for database basics. Communication and Kenyan introductory-law video gaps remain.

Intermediate: start MA prerequisites before PM, use FM for core finance, FR for reporting and AA for audit principles. KRA tutorials add eTIMS operations only. Company law and comprehensive current Kenyan taxation lectures remain gaps.

Advanced: use SBR for reporting judgements, NYU corporate finance/valuation for AFM, APM for performance strategy, AAA for audit judgement, SBL for leadership, and PSASB for public-sector transition topics. Standards-sensitive content needs current-source comparison. Advanced Kenyan taxation, CPA integrated cases and official workshop completion remain unresolved.

## Rights and verification

OpenTuition prohibits video downloading; its OpenLicense concerns printed material. MIT and Harvard courses carry non-commercial licence restrictions. NYU, RCM, PSASB and KRA recordings have no established commercial rehosting grant in this review. KRA site terms also restrict automated extraction, so recurring harvesting is excluded. Question text, solutions and AI uses need separate permission review. Ordinary source links carry no copying licence.

YouTube oEmbed resolved 18 sampled IDs, including two RCM-linked examples. That checks current metadata, not complete playback, geographic availability or video bytes. PSASB exposes a public introduction player while enrolled lessons are gated. KRA sample IDs were read from the official tutorial index; playback was not verified. No course package, link or transcript is counted as acquired video.
