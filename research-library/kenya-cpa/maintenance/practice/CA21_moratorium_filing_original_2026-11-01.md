# CA21 — Original pre-insolvency moratorium documentation practice (2026-11-01)

**Status:** newly authored fictional teaching cases; not KASNEB examination questions or legal advice. Primary legal provisions selectively checked; current consolidated law, 2026 amendments and independent Kenyan insolvency-law/academic review **pending**.

## Case 1 — Incomplete filing pack
Mwangaza Fabricators Ltd (fictional) asks whether a temporary moratorium automatically follows from a directors' board minute and a projected cash-flow spreadsheet. No licensed monitor has consented, no financial-position statement identifies secured and unsecured creditors, and nothing has been lodged with the court.

**Task:** Identify the missing categories of evidence and whether creditor protection can be assumed.

**Worked answer:** A board minute and cash-flow projection are not, on their own, the prescribed filing pack. The applicable primary rules call for a structured statement of financial position, relevant secured/preferential/unsecured and connected-party details, directors' eligibility statement, monitor consent and opinion, and court lodgement/recognition. A credible cash-flow forecast also does not establish statutory eligibility. Do not assume a moratorium exists or that every creditor remedy is barred.

## Case 2 — Age of financial information
A fictional company's directors have a financial-position statement dated seven days before intended lodgement. A second statement is three weeks old and the monitor has not approved the older date. A third is three months old.

**Task:** Apply the timing safeguards in the original 2021 Fourth Schedule without claiming they exhaust current law.

**Worked answer:** Seven days is within the default two-week recency window. The three-week statement falls outside that default and needs the legally permitted monitor justification/exception, rather than automatic acceptance. Three months exceeds the stated outer two-month exception in the 2021 schedule. The dates are illustrative and must be reassessed under the current instrument and the relevant date of lodgement.

## Case 3 — Monitor opinion and court acknowledgement
A licensed monitor has received the required documentation. The directors assume that the monitor may wait indefinitely to give an opinion and that a draft form alone stops enforcement.

**Task:** State the procedural checks to perform.

**Worked answer:** Under the inspected 2021 notice, the monitor's statement is to be supplied within 14 days after submission of requisite documentation, and the monitor-opinion document is to be lodged within ten business days after it is supplied to the directors. The court's endorsement and prescribed Form 38 recognition are distinct from an unfiled draft. Check the current consolidated rules, exact filing events, court practice and exclusions before applying these periods to a real company.
