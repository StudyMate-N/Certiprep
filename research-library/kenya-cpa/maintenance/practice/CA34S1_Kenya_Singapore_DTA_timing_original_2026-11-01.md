# CA34S1 — Original international-tax treaty timing exercises (1 November 2026)

**Original fictional educational problems, not KASNEB past papers.** All amounts are hypothetical. Use the [ratified Kenya–Singapore DTA](https://www.iras.gov.sg/docs/default-source/dtas/singapore-kenya-dta-%28ratified%29%2820-apr-2026%29.pdf), particularly Articles 10, 11, 13, 28 and 29. No treaty text, tables, figures or third-party questions are reproduced. The treaty was signed 23 September 2024, entered into force 20 April 2026, but its withholding provisions **take effect in Kenya from 1 January 2027**. Do not equate entry into force with the start of treaty relief.

## Case 1 — Timing gate
A fictional Kenyan company pays a Singapore-resident investor a gross dividend of KES 1,250,000 on 15 December 2026. The same payment is considered again, hypothetically, on 15 January 2027.

**Question:** Is the 8% treaty ceiling available by reason of the 2024 treaty in December 2026? What is the **maximum treaty source-tax ceiling**, *if all eligibility conditions are met*, for the January 2027 payment?

**Worked response:** No. Article 29(2)(a)(i) defers Kenya withholding effect until 1 January 2027. December 2026 tax requires the then-applicable domestic law and any other applicable relief, which this exercise does not compute. For the hypothetical January 2027 payment, Article 10(2) ceiling = KES 1,250,000 × 0.08 = **KES 100,000**. This is a **ceiling**, not a claim that 8% must always be withheld: verify residency, beneficial ownership, permanent-establishment connection, principal-purpose test, and any lower domestic-law rate.

## Case 2 — Interest and technical fees
A fictional Kenyan enterprise has qualifying January 2027 payments to Singapore residents: KES 600,000 interest and KES 750,000 technical fees. Assume treaty entitlement and all relevant legal conditions are satisfied.

**Question:** Calculate the treaty maximum source-tax ceilings, not the actual amounts payable.

**Worked response:** Article 11(2) interest ceiling = KES 600,000 × 0.10 = **KES 60,000**. Article 13(2) technical-fee ceiling = KES 750,000 × 0.10 = **KES 75,000**. Combined hypothetical ceilings = **KES 135,000**. Check that payments meet the treaty definitions, do not fall under exceptions, and satisfy the anti-abuse rule. Actual withholding may be lower under domestic law or a specific exemption.
