# CA12 original practice — meeting agenda, action minutes and team communication

**Created:** 2026-11-02 UTC. **Scope:** Foundation CA12 Communication Skills, June 2024 syllabus comparator; April 2027 revised syllabus not yet mapped. **Authorship:** original fictional scenario and model response; no KASNEB or third-party exam question copied. **Review:** internal logic checked; independent subject-expert/examiner review pending. **Not a past paper.**

## Fictional case

The four-person **Mwangaza Student Enterprise** team meets online on 10 November 2026, 09:00–09:30 EAT. Attendees: Amina (chair), Brian (finance), Carol (marketing) and David (secretary). Purpose: approve the December student-market budget and resolve late weekly sales reports. Brian proposes KES 24,000; Amina asks for a reduction; the team unanimously approves **KES 20,000**, subject to Brian circulating a revised line-item budget by **12 November 2026, 17:00 EAT**. The team also agrees that Carol will circulate a one-page sales-report template by **11 November 2026, 17:00 EAT**, and each team member will submit Friday sales figures by **17:00 EAT every Friday**, starting **13 November 2026**. The next meeting is 17 November 2026 at 09:00 EAT.

### Tasks

1. Draft a concise agenda suitable for circulation before the meeting.
2. Write action-focused minutes recording the decisions, responsible persons and due dates. Do not invent votes or participants.
3. State which of the team's communications should be synchronous and which can be asynchronous, giving a reason for each.

## Model response (original)

**Agenda — 10 November 2026, 09:00–09:30 EAT, online:** (1) Opening and attendance; (2) December student-market budget decision; (3) Weekly sales reporting and deadlines; (4) Action owners and next meeting; (5) Close.

**Action minutes:** Present: Amina (chair), Brian, Carol and David (secretary). The team considered a proposed KES 24,000 December budget and unanimously approved a reduced ceiling of **KES 20,000**. **Action 1:** Brian to circulate the revised itemised budget by **12 November 2026 at 17:00 EAT**. The team agreed to standardise weekly sales reporting. **Action 2:** Carol to circulate the one-page reporting template by **11 November 2026 at 17:00 EAT**. **Action 3:** All members to submit Friday sales figures by **17:00 EAT each Friday**, starting **13 November 2026**. Next meeting: **17 November 2026, 09:00 EAT**, online.

**Communication choice:** The budget negotiation and any high-stakes disagreement benefit from synchronous video discussion so questions and objections can be resolved immediately. The approved minutes, template and routine sales figures can be circulated asynchronously by email or a shared workspace to preserve a clear written record and accommodate different schedules.

## Internal checks and limitations

- Approved ceiling is **KES 20,000**, not the original KES 24,000 proposal; no arithmetic beyond comparison is required.
- Four named attendees; the only vote explicitly described is unanimous budget approval. No motion seconder, quorum requirement, signature, or statutory board-minute claim is invented.
- Actions have owner and date/time; the recurring Friday deadline starts on Friday **13 November 2026**.
- The source transcript from York University was consulted for generic concepts of facilitator/minute-taker, action records and synchronous/asynchronous channels, **not copied** into this exercise.
- This is a practice exemplar, not a legal compliance template, KASNEB question, licensed recording or independently validated marking scheme. Kenyan professional/board meeting formalities require separate review.
