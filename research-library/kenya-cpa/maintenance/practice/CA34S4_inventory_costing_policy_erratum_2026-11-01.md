# Policy clarification for CA34S4_original_inventory_costing.md — 2026-11-01

The earlier original exercise correctly calculates **both** FIFO and periodic weighted-average outcomes, but **must not** be presented as demonstrating two equally adopted costing policies for Kenyan government entities.

**New primary-source finding:** The National Treasury's *Accounting Policies of the Public Sector* explicitly selects **FIFO** for the government, while the PSASB's 31-page *IPSAS 12 Inventories Guideline* explains that the standard generally permits FIFO and weighted average for interchangeable inventories. The general IPSAS option does not itself supersede a narrower applicable government policy. Exact entity/financial-year applicability still needs a specialist check.

**Editorial disposition:** Preserve the original exercises and arithmetic. For Kenyan government-policy applications, label FIFO as the stated Treasury election; label weighted average as conceptual comparison only pending entity-specific confirmation. Continue to distinguish cost-flow arithmetic from subsequent measurement at lower of cost and current replacement cost for no-charge distribution, or lower of cost and NRV for sale.

Sources: https://www.treasury.go.ke/sites/default/files/Latest%20updates/ACCOUN~2.PDF (printed pp. 30–31); https://www.psasb.go.ke/Documents/Accounting%20Standards/Guidelines/IPSAS%2012%20%20Inventories%20Guideline.pdf (printed pp. 6–8).
