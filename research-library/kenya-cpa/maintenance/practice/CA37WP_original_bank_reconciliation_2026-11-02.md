# CA37WP original bank reconciliation — 2 November 2026

**Status:** original fictional exercise for independent practice, not an official KASNEB/ICPAK work-simulation task, not a QuickBooks video solution, and not independently subject-expert reviewed. Currency: Kenyan shillings (KES).

## Scenario

A fictional business, Lakeview Stationery Ltd, has a bank-statement balance of **KES 162,200** and an unreconciled cash-book balance of **KES 154,800** on 31 October 2026. Deposits in transit: KES 18,000; unpresented cheques: KES 21,400; bank charge not in books: KES 1,500; direct customer bank transfer not in books: KES 7,500; interest credited by bank: KES 1,000; dishonoured customer cheque: KES 3,000.

**Tasks:** (1) reconcile the statement and cash-book balances; (2) identify which adjustments require ledger entries; (3) explain how a learner might verify transactions and record the adjustments in accounting software, without relying on a specific edition's UI.

## Independently recalculated worked answer

**Bank side:** KES 162,200 + KES 18,000 − KES 21,400 = **KES 158,800**.

**Cash-book side:** KES 154,800 + KES 7,500 + KES 1,000 − KES 1,500 − KES 3,000 = **KES 158,800**.

Deposits in transit and unpresented cheques are timing differences: they do **not** require additional cash-book postings if already recorded correctly. Record the direct customer transfer and interest receipt as bank debits with corresponding appropriate income/receivable credits; record the bank charge as an expense debit/bank credit; reverse or reinstate the receivable for the dishonoured cheque as appropriate to the original entry. Check source documents, bank statement and customer ledger before posting; do not duplicate previously recorded items.
