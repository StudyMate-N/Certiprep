## Case 1 — expense evidence classification
A fictional company records five 2025 costs: local supplies A KES 120,000 (valid transmitted eTIMS invoice with buyer PIN), local supplies B KES 85,000 (likewise), local supplies C KES 20,000 (supplier invoice with missing buyer PIN and no documented exception), salaries KES 60,000, interest KES 15,000.

**Task:** Reconcile the book total, identify electronic-invoice-supported costs, legally excluded categories and an exception/evidence investigation queue.

**Worked solution:** Book costs = 120,000 + 85,000 + 20,000 + 60,000 + 15,000 = **KES 300,000**. eTIMS-supported local supplies = **KES 205,000**. Salaries plus interest = **KES 75,000**, categories excluded from e-invoice requirement under regulation 10. The remaining **KES 20,000** requires buyer-PIN correction, evidence and statutory-exception investigation. Reconciliation: 205,000 + 75,000 + 20,000 = 300,000. **Do not automatically label KES 20,000 tax-disallowed**; tax deductibility requires a separate legal/factual decision.

## Case 2 — credit-note linkage
A fictional supplier issued an original valid invoice for KES 175,000, followed by a KES 25,000 credit note referencing that invoice. The buyer's books show KES 165,000 as net expense.

**Worked solution:** Net invoice evidence = 175,000 − 25,000 = **KES 150,000**. Book-to-evidence difference = 165,000 − 150,000 = **KES 15,000**, requiring review. Regulation 7(2) requires a credit/debit note to refer to the original invoice. A mismatch is a reconciliation flag, not a conclusion about fraud or deductibility.

## Case 3 — CGT arithmetic, not deadline advice
Fictional non-exempt transfer: value KES 8,000,000, incidental transfer costs KES 160,000, acquisition cost KES 4,500,000, acquisition incidental costs KES 90,000, enhancement KES 250,000.

**Worked solution:** Net transfer proceeds = 8,000,000 − 160,000 = **KES 7,840,000**. Adjusted cost = 4,500,000 + 90,000 + 250,000 = **KES 4,840,000**. Net gain = **KES 3,000,000**. At the KRA currently stated 15% rate, illustrative CGT = **KES 450,000**. This assumes costs are qualifying and no exemption, loss adjustment, valuation or other special rule applies. **Do not teach a CGT payment due date from this exercise:** KRA's legacy companies page conflicts with its current CGT page and relevant court-law questions remain unresolved.
