# Original CA32 / CA23 practice — group consolidation (2 Nov 2026)

**Original synthetic exercise; not a copied KASNEB or publisher question.** For independent academic review; no paper certified complete.

Kibo Holdings acquired 75% of Nuru Ltd on 1 January 20X6 for KES 210,000. At acquisition Nuru's identifiable net assets had fair value KES 240,000. Assume no previously held interest, no transaction costs in consideration, no contingent consideration, and no unrecognised fair-value adjustments. The group elects to measure NCI at its proportionate share of identifiable net assets. At 31 December 20X6 Nuru's net assets are KES 300,000, entirely attributable to post-acquisition retained profit increase of KES 60,000; no dividends, impairment or other comprehensive income. Kibo has an intra-group receivable of KES 12,000, exactly matched by Nuru's payable.

**Tasks:** (a) compute acquisition-date NCI; (b) compute goodwill; (c) compute closing NCI; (d) compute parent's share of post-acquisition retained earnings; (e) explain the intercompany balance elimination.

## Worked solution and independent arithmetic check

- Acquisition-date NCI = 25% × 240,000 = **KES 60,000**.
- Goodwill = consideration 210,000 + acquisition NCI 60,000 − identifiable net assets 240,000 = **KES 30,000**.
- Closing NCI = 25% × (240,000 + 60,000) = **KES 75,000**.
- Parent share of Nuru post-acquisition retained earnings = 75% × 60,000 = **KES 45,000**.
- Eliminate intra-group receivable KES 12,000 against payable KES 12,000 in full; consolidated assets and liabilities each fall by KES 12,000, with no effect on net assets.

Cross-check: closing NCI 75,000 = acquisition NCI 60,000 + 25% × 60,000 = 75,000; 75% × 60,000 + 25% × 60,000 = 60,000.
