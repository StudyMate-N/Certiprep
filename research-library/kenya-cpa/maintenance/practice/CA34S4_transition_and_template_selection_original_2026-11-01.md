# CA34S4 / CA26 — Kenya public-sector transition and template-selection practice
Original synthetic teaching exercise • 2026-11-01 • Not an official KASNEB question

**Scope.** Transitional financial reporting for the year ended 30 June 2026, and choosing an entity-specific PSASB annual template. For learning only. No claim that the detailed KASNEB examinable outcome or sitting has been independently locked.

## Case 1 — Minimum transitional recognition (fictional)
A county executive preparing its 30 June 2026 statements has these independently substantiated closing amounts:
- Cash at bank: KES 1,200,000
- Receivables: KES 400,000
- Treasury bills (financial asset): KES 150,000
- Inventory available for service delivery: KES 180,000 (assume compliant IPSAS measurement already verified)
- Trade payables: KES 320,000
- Land valuation estimate: KES 2,000,000, **not yet sufficiently evidenced for recognition**.

**Questions:** (a) Identify the reporting phase. (b) Compute the subtotal of verified assets, liabilities and residual net assets for this deliberately simplified closing position. (c) Should the unverified land estimate be forced into the subtotal because it is Year 2?

### Worked answer
(a) FY2025/26 is Year 2 of the 2024–2027 roadmap. For transitioning national/county governments, the roadmap requires financial assets, liabilities and inventories as the minimum; supported non-current assets may be included ahead of Year 3. This is not a universal rule for entities already reporting full IPSAS accrual or IFRS.
(b) Verified assets = 1,200,000 + 400,000 + 150,000 + 180,000 = **KES 1,930,000**. Verified liabilities = **KES 320,000**. Simplified residual = 1,930,000 − 320,000 = **KES 1,610,000**.
(c) **No.** Year 2 does not justify inserting an unsupported land valuation. Recognition/measurement and any opening balance adjustments require separate evidence and the entity's applicable reporting instructions. The residual is a pedagogical arithmetic subtotal, **not a complete audited statement of financial position**.

## Case 2 — Entity-specific annual template selection
A county executive, a regulatory state corporation applying IPSAS accrual, and a commercial state corporation applying IFRS each prepare annual FY2025/26 financial statements. Choose the appropriate **category** on the PSASB index, not a universal generic template.

### Worked answer
- County executive: **Counties Reporting Template — Executive, Assembly, CRF and ROR → Annual Template for County Executive and Assemblies FY 2025-26**, subject to exact entity and filing guidance.
- Regulatory state corporation on IPSAS accrual: **State Corporations and SAGAs → Annual IPSAS Accrual Template for SC, SAGAs, Funds FY2025-26**, subject to its prescribed reporting framework.
- Commercial state corporation on IFRS: **State Corporations and SAGAs → Annual IFRS Reporting Template for SC Revised FY2025-26**, subject to its prescribed reporting framework.
These are index-level selections; the individual DOCX templates were **not downloaded or inspected**. Do not claim their internal schedules or embedded formulas have been verified.

## Case 3 — Identify a source-version trap
The PSASB FAQ currently says national and county governments shall apply IPSAS cash basis. A student cites this to claim inventories are not mandatory for FY2025/26 transitioning entities. Evaluate.

### Worked answer
**Not defensible as a current blanket statement.** The FAQ appears to preserve an earlier framework summary. The official 2024–2027 transition roadmap and the PSASB 3 August 2026 template announcement establish phased accrual reporting and mandatory inventory recognition in the FY2025/26 Year 2 transitional reporting requirements. Identify the entity, reporting period and applicable circular/template rather than using the undated FAQ as the controlling authority. Do not infer that every public-sector entity shares the same transition phase.
