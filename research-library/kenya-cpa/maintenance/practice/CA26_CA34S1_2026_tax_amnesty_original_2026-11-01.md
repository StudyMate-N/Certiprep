## Case 1 — Qualifying principal fully paid (fictional)
A business has an agreed **2025** domestic tax principal of KES 240,000, interest of KES 12,000 and penalties of KES 18,000. It files all required returns and pays the entire principal on 20 November 2026. Ignore any other debt, disputes or special statutory exclusions.

**Question:** How much principal is payable and what is the potential waiver?

**Worked answer:** Principal **KES 240,000** remains payable. Eligible interest plus penalties = 12,000 + 18,000 = **KES 30,000** potentially waived after full principal settlement by 31 December 2026, subject to actual KRA account verification. The amnesty does not waive principal. Total hypothetical ledger before waiver = KES 270,000; after eligible waiver = KES 240,000.

## Case 2 — Incomplete payment and period allocation
A taxpayer owes **KES 100,000** principal for a single agreed 2025 tax period and pays KES 80,000 by 31 December 2026. Interest and penalties total KES 15,000. There are no separately settled tax periods.

**Question:** Can the taxpayer claim an automatic 80% waiver?

**Worked answer:** **No.** The KRA notice conditions waiver on **full** qualifying principal settlement by the deadline. Remaining principal = 100,000 − 80,000 = **KES 20,000**. Do not invent a proportional KES 12,000 waiver. KRA's guidance allows separately fully settled periods to qualify; that scenario is not presented here. Verify the actual ledger and relevant legal rules before applying this to a real taxpayer.

## Case 3 — Post-2025 liability and late filing
(A) A taxpayer has KES 50,000 principal and KES 3,000 penalties for a **2026** domestic tax period. (B) Another taxpayer has an unfiled **2025** nil return and a late-filing penalty, with no principal tax outstanding, and files the return during the 2026 amnesty window.

**Worked answer:** (A) The 2026 liability is outside this amnesty; **KES 53,000** remains due on the stated simplified facts, subject to other independent reliefs. (B) The pre-2026 nil-return late-filing penalty may qualify for automatic amnesty once the return is filed, subject to KRA processing. Do not assume all tax heads qualify: EACCMA customs duties are excluded.
