## A. CA14 externality and welfare (hypothetical)

A hypothetical local market has marginal benefit MB(Q)=140−2Q, marginal private cost MPC(Q)=20+2Q and marginal external cost MEC(Q)=Q, where Q is daily service units and all marginal values are KES per unit.

**Tasks:** Find unregulated output, socially efficient output, corrective per-unit charge at the efficient output, and deadweight loss from unregulated production.

**Solution:** Unregulated MB=MPC gives 140−2Q=20+2Q, so Q=30. Marginal social cost MSC=20+3Q. MB=MSC gives 140−2Q=20+3Q, so Q*=24. Corrective charge MEC(24)=KES 24/unit. The excess-output loss is the area between MSC and MB over Q=24..30: integral of (5Q−120)dQ = 0.5×(6)×(30)=KES 90/day. At Q=30 the marginal social cost is KES 110, marginal benefit KES 80, difference KES 30; at Q=24 they coincide. This is an illustrative Pigouvian analysis, not a Kenyan levy.

## B. CA33 bond valuation (hypothetical)

A bond pays a KES 8,000 coupon annually for three years and repays KES 100,000 face value at year 3. Required effective annual yield is 10%.

**Task:** Price the bond immediately after a coupon date, assuming no accrued interest or credit default.

**Solution:** Price = 8,000/1.10 + 8,000/(1.10)^2 + 108,000/(1.10)^3 = **KES 95,026.30** (rounded). Price is below face because coupon rate 8% is below required yield 10%. Check: discount each cash flow separately; this is not a current market quote.
