## Accounting and assurance

Use the applicable period and required-versus-issued edition. IFRS 18 and IFRS 19 have 1 January 2027 effective dates; issued standards are not automatically examinable in a particular KASNEB sitting. Primary navigator: https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ .

The 2026 IAASB handbook includes requirements with later effective dates; identify the specific standard and reporting period before using it. The 2026 IESBA code has staged changes, including December 2026 and July 2028 provisions. The 2026 IPSAS handbook covers pronouncements published as at 31 January 2026; handbook publication is not proof of Kenya-specific implementation or examination scope. These copyrighted handbooks are access links, not archive media.

Kenyan sustainability adoption and the Treasury cash-to-accrual transition need local implementation guidance. The ICPAK roadmap filename includes Draft; corroborate it against later notices. ## Tax and law

The acquired Finance Act 2026 Gazette scan is an amendment Act, not the full current tax code. Its staged commencement must be read provision by provision. KRA's July 2026-labelled Income Tax Act PDF expressly lists Finance Act 2026 as outstanding/unapplied; it cannot by itself support a claim of complete current consolidation. KRA's Excise Duty Act file named 2026 contains July 2025 text. The TPA compilation has a September 2026 as-at date. These specific compiled PDFs carry NC restrictions and are excluded from commercial redistribution.

Current-year law also requires checking amendments outside the annual Finance Act and applicable court orders. October 2026 parliamentary reporting on Tax Laws Amendment legislation is an unresolved follow-up: Bill passage does not itself prove assent, Gazette text or commencement. A guessed KRA direct PDF URL returned 404. The research did not secure the enacted October instrument, so no new current petroleum VAT calculation is certified.

The one-page KRA-hosted Legal Notice 128 explicitly applies only 15 July–14 October 2026, although its search listing shows a late-October timestamp. Document text, Gazette date and sunset provision control over search metadata. It is rejected as a current November rate source.

KRA's 22 October notice states 9% for Q4 fringe-benefit/deemed-interest purposes. The separate July notice includes an 8% half-year low-interest-benefit reference. Distinguish statutory purpose and applicable dates; do not mix the rates. All rate references remain link-only, dated facts requiring recheck.

PSASB's file named The-Public-Finance-Management-Act-No.-18-of-2012.pdf is actually a 22-page 2016 County Audit Committee Guideline. Its identity was checked from its text and it is rejected as the Act. PSASB's Public Audit Act file is a genuine 41-page original 2015 Gazette enactment, but no full current-amendment/court review was completed, so it remains a dated original access point.
