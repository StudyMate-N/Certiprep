# Exact-edition rights and question-use audit

Reviewed 1 November 2026 UTC. This is a documented acquisition decision, not a guarantee of title or a judicial determination. Each work keeps its own licence. No archive-wide commercial, question-bank or AI licence is asserted.

## What changed and what remains valid

1. **Current versus earlier editions.** Current OpenStax guidance specifies NC-SA and a commercial permission route. The specific LOC-deposited 2022 macroeconomics artifact instead carries CC BY 4.0 in-file and in the item Rights Advisory. Its exact source hash and adaptation are retained; later files cannot inherit that decision. [Current guidance](https://help.openstax.org/s/article/Licensing-information-of-OpenStax-textbooks), [deposited item](https://www.loc.gov/item/2021387735/).
2. **CC is conditional, not casually revocable.** CC BY 4.0 distinguishes the exact licensed material, licence conditions, breach/termination and cure. Later different distribution terms do not automatically terminate a compliant earlier grant. The licensor can grant only rights it controls. Access contracts and unlicensed components must be checked separately. [Legal code, sections 2, 3 and 6](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode).
3. **Kenya Law access and compilations.** Current terms restrict commercial redistribution, bulk extraction and AI training without an agreement and identify NC reuse of raw documents. No bulk harvesting or commercial reuse of its current service is approved here. A narrower statutory raw-law basis does not license editorial additions, logos or database services. [Current terms](https://kenyalaw.org/terms-of-use/).
4. **IFRS permissions can expire.** Educational training terms are institution/use-specific, require current materials and expire when withdrawn/updated. Commercial product reuse and other users need the appropriate licence. No IFRS standard or training-question PDF is redistributed. [Primary terms](https://www.ifrs.org/legal/education-material-licensing/).
5. **Exam questions and solutions.** KASNEB section 7 requires prior written consent for content reproduction/modification/distribution. An e-library fee, public past-paper link or instructor access is not an established republication grant. Third-party solutions can have separate rights. Publicly expressed mathematical/accounting methods can support independently authored teaching, but copying question wording, scenarios, arrangements or solutions requires a supported rights basis. No KASNEB question PDFs or third-party solution bank are distributed. [KASNEB terms](https://www.kasneb.or.ke/terms-conditions).
6. **Component-level remediation.** Accounting Volume 1 source pages 248-260 are omitted because section 6.6 is separately NC and explicitly removable. The OR source's mixed-credit metro page and unresolved photographs/maps are omitted; the MIT component notice remains. Microeconomics, macroeconomics, PEFA and raw-law cover adaptations have their own evidence. Derivative hashes and omissions are recorded; original retained text was checked. This does not clear unrelated third-party content.
7. **Quality is independent of permission.** Accounting Volume 2 remains on accuracy hold despite its open licence. Its source physical page 240 uses a general IFRS lessee fair-value cap inconsistent with IFRS 16 initial-measurement requirements. It needs qualified correction/review before distribution. The OR variable-index typo is disclosed rather than silently corrected in the source; selected optimisations were independently solved.
8. **Technology does not resolve all rights.** OCR, translation, format conversion or AI rewriting does not establish permission. Model training, RAG retrieval/output and generated-question reuse require their own applicable-law/use review. The project claims no general clearance for those uses. [CC analysis](https://creativecommons.org/2025/05/15/understanding-cc-licenses-and-ai-training-a-legal-primer/).

## How the audit is recorded

Use rights/rights-register.csv for artifact evidence and rights/usage-rights-by-action.csv for use-specific decisions. Dated primary observations are in rights/current-primary-observations.json. They are web observations, not full-page snapshots; no full-source hash is fabricated for them. Exact downloaded source and derivative hashes remain in acquisitions and request provenance. Publisher notices are evidence, not independent warranties; confidence and remaining review limits remain visible.

Recheck an affected resource on a new edition/hash, source access-term change, permission expiry, rights-holder claim, discovered component conflict, new proposed use or condition breach. Keep the last defensible working version while it remains permitted and useful. Exclude conflicted files from distribution, retain the decision history, and do not bulk-delete working material merely because a website has changed.
